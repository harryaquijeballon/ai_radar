### 1. Right Order, Wrong Scale: Auditing LLM Judges for Occupational AI Measurement (academic, Harry Lyu and Neil Thompson, 2026-10-01)

**Why it matters:** A direct validity audit of LLM-as-judge: 25 of 33 judge configurations reach pair accuracy of at least 0.60, yet their estimated acceptance rates span 3.0%–97.9% against a 61.1% human reference, fine-tuning improves ordering while reducing agreement with workers, and a TF-IDF baseline nearly matches the best judge. Builders should validate judges against the aggregates they will report, not just ranking agreement. Figures are from the abstract; full text not read.
