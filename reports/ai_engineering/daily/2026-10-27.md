### 1. Silent and Costly: Characterizing and Detecting Prompt-Caching Mis-Integration in LLM Agents (academic, Ying Zhang, Ruoqi Guo, Yi Liu, Gelei Deng, Yuekang Li, Yepang Liu, Leo Zhang, 2026-10-26)

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34817 · **Library entry:** [2026-zhang-cachelint-prompt-caching-mis-integration](../../../library/entries/2026-zhang-cachelint-prompt-caching-mis-integration.md)

### 2. Testing with Coding Agents: A Large-Scale Comparison of Agent-Supported and Human-Authored Pull Requests (academic, Roberto Milanese, Francesco Salzano, Hovhannes Muradyan, Jingjing Wu, Remo Pareschi, Fausto Fasano, Mattia Fazzini, 2026-10-26)

**Why it matters:** Gives teams adopting coding agents a statistically controlled, large-sample baseline for how agent assistance actually changes testing behaviour, rather than anecdote.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35297 · **Library entry:** [2026-milanese-agent-pull-request-testing-comparison](../../../library/entries/2026-milanese-agent-pull-request-testing-comparison.md)
**Selected because:** 114,134 pull requests across 391 repositories with mixed-effects modelling — a measured, transferable finding on AI-assisted software development (lens 7).

### 3. How Do Coding-Agent Harnesses Self-Evolve? An Empirical Study of Retrospective Adaptation (academic, Christoph Treude, Sebastian Baltes, 2026-10-26)

**Why it matters:** Direct evidence that letting an agent revise its own harness is not reliably net-positive — only 2 of 7 verifiable fixes actually eliminated the targeted failure — a concrete argument for gating harness self-modification behind its own evaluation step.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34938 · **Library entry:** [2026-treude-baltes-coding-agent-harness-self-evolution](../../../library/entries/2026-treude-baltes-coding-agent-harness-self-evolution.md)
**Selected because:** A controlled empirical test of a named self-evolving-harness mechanism (Claude Code's \retro skill) with a quantified failure mode — core to harness and context engineering (lens 2).
