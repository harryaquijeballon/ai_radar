### 1. Latency Scaling Differences for GPT and Claude Models (academic, Jason Li — Epoch AI, 2026-09-08)

**Why it matters:** An independent, methodologically transparent benchmark of how time-to-first-token scales with context length for GPT-5.6 Terra, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5, and Claude Opus 5 — GPT-5.6 models show a statistically significant quadratic latency component while Claude models stay near-linear. ### 2. Hidden in Plain Text: LLM-Assisted Detection of Discriminatory Municipal Laws (academic, Yasmine Mabene, Daniel E. Ho, Dan Bateyko, Derek Ouyang — Stanford RegLab / ICAIL 2026, 2026-09-08)

**Why it matters:** A staged LLM pipeline (flag → classify → prioritize → human-expert filter) scanned ~9 million local-law sections across 9,623 U.S. jurisdictions and was checked against human annotators (>90% accuracy, 88% agreement on "high priority" flags) — a rare AI-for-research pipeline reported with real validation metrics and explicit stated limits, not just a demo.
