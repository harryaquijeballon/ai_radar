### 1. Cracks in the Foundation: Seemingly Minor Architectural Choices Impact Long Context Extension (academic, Amanda Bertsch, Luca Soldaini, Matthew R. Gormley, Graham Neubig, Hannaneh Hajishirzi, Kyle Lo, Dirk Groeneveld — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-10; COLM 2026)

**Why it matters:** A rigorous, large-scale ablation (170,000+ GPU hours, 26 released 7B models) shows four individually-minor transformer architecture choices compound to cut long-context performance by up to 47% — and the damage is invisible to short-context loss, the metric most pretraining pipelines actually watch. A concrete checklist for anyone building or selecting a long-context model for document-grounded research or policy work.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10296 · **Library entry:** [2026-bertsch-cracks-foundation-long-context](../../../library/entries/2026-bertsch-cracks-foundation-long-context.md)

### 2. Actions Speak Louder than Words: Measuring Cross-Lingual Policy Retention in Tool-Using Agents (academic, Sourabrata Mukherjee, Kalika Bali, Sunayana Sitaram — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-11; COLM 2026)

**Why it matters:** A 2.38M-rollout evaluation across 8 models, 6 benchmarks, and 41 languages finds frontier tool-using agents retain only ~71-73% of their action policy across languages, and separately shows common trace-extraction measurement artifacts can distort reported agent performance by up to 26x — two directly actionable findings for anyone designing an agent evaluation harness, not just for multilingual use cases.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11110 · **Library entry:** [2026-mukherjee-cross-lingual-policy-retention-agents](../../../library/entries/2026-mukherjee-cross-lingual-policy-retention-agents.md)
**Selected because:** High on lenses 3 (tool use) and 4 (evaluation) — quantified reliability gap plus a named measurement pitfall.

### 3. Why Does CLAUDE.md Keep Growing? Catastrophic Remembering in Agentic Coding (academic, Kushal Chakrabarti — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-11)

**Why it matters:** Studies why agent-instruction files (CLAUDE.md-style) expand indefinitely across 1,867 real repositories (247,694 instruction instances, 200%+ growth), names the mechanism ("catastrophic remembering" — deleting an instruction without its rationale risks a correctness regression), and shows a cheap fix: attaching explanatory comments removed 99.3% of excess instructions and improved instruction-following accuracy by up to 23.1%. Directly applicable to this project's own harness maintenance.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11095 · **Library entry:** [2026-chakrabarti-claude-md-catastrophic-remembering](../../../library/entries/2026-chakrabarti-claude-md-catastrophic-remembering.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 2 (harness and context engineering) — a named failure mode with a quantified, cheap mitigation.
