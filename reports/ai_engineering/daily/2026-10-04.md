### 1. Agent Evaluation Reliability: More Tasks Won't (Always) Fix An Agent Leaderboard (academic, Hardy, Azam, Reuel, Kochenderfer, Koyejo, 2026-09-30)

**Why it matters:** Shows that agent-leaderboard rankings can reflect scaffold choice rather than the model: fixed model–scaffold systems rank at 0.935–0.994 reliability, but underlying-model reliability is only 0.148–0.841. Pooling diverse benchmarks raised projected cross-task reliability from 0.44 to 0.75 at equal cost, so evaluation budget is better spent on the dominant uncertainty than on more similar tasks.
