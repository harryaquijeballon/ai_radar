### 3. Just a rumour of a bug is enough to find a security exploit these days (commentary, Simon Willison relaying Anil Madhavapeddy and Nick Craig-Wood, 2026-08-28)

**Why it matters:** Two independent, named maintainers give quantified, corroborating evidence that coding-agent-assisted exploit discovery (exploits found within minutes of a patch hint; 40 vs. 20 security disclosures per rclone in a month vs. a decade) has outpaced vulnerability-disclosure infrastructure — CVE assignment delays have grown from 2-3 days to 3-4 weeks. A measurable shift in the threat model around embargoed disclosure that any maintainer with a public issue tracker should account for.
