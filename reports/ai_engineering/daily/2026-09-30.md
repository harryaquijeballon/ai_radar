## Today's developments

### 1. ProvenanceGuard: Source-Aware Verification for MCP Agents (academic, Ander Alvarez et al., Multiverse Computing, 2026-09-29)

**Why it matters:** Agents that cite tool outputs can be right about a fact yet name the wrong source, which undermines auditability in research and policy products. ProvenanceGuard treats attribution as its own verification axis: log MCP calls with stable source IDs, split answers into claims, route each claim to its source, and check both support and the named source. Reported results (block F1 0.802, source accuracy 0.858) come from the authors' medical-domain traces, so pilot it on your own traces first.

**Source:** https://huggingface.co/blog/MultiverseComputingCAI/getting-the-source-right-not-just-the-fact-source · **Library entry:** [2026-alvarez-provenanceguard-source-aware-verification](../../../library/entries/2026-alvarez-provenanceguard-source-aware-verification.md)
**Selected because:** Lenses 3, 4 and 8: deterministic claim-level attribution checks over MCP traces, directly transferable to research agents.
