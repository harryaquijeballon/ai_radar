### 2. AgentChaos: Chaos Engineering for Agent Systems via Programmatic Fault Injection (academic, Gou Tan, Zhensu Sun, Jieke Shi, Ting Zhang, Zilong He, Qingfu Wu, Shuai Liang, Weifeng Sun, Junda He, Pengfei Chen, Chuanfu Zhang, Lwin Khin Shar, David Lo — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-07)

**Why it matters:** A directly reusable methodology — HTTP-layer, non-intrusive fault injection any team can point at their own agent stack — plus a generalizable, actionable finding: agent robustness depends on system implementation, not model capability, an argument for investing in agent-level fault handling rather than assuming a stronger model will be more robust.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06790 · **Library entry:** [2026-tan-agentchaos-fault-injection](../../../library/entries/2026-tan-agentchaos-fault-injection.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 5/6 (observability, reliability) — a reusable fault-injection framework with a quantified, generalizable finding.

### 3. Online Monitoring and Corrective Steering of Programming Agents (academic, Shuyang Liu, Saman Dehghan, Ji Young Kim, Jatin Ganhotra, Martin Hirzel, Reyhaneh Jabbarvand — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-07)

**Why it matters:** A reusable architectural pattern for long-lived coding or tool-use agents: catch drift and repeated-failure loops with a cheap, deterministic rule-based monitor, and reserve the LLM's judgment for the moments the rules flag — evaluated on SWE-bench with a quantified gain (up to 15.2%) at minimal added cost.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06701 · **Library entry:** [2026-liu-liveplan-programming-agent-monitoring](../../../library/entries/2026-liu-liveplan-programming-agent-monitoring.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 1/5 (agent architecture, observability) — a concrete, evaluable design with a stated cost trade-off.
