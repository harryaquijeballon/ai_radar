## Today's developments

### 1. No More Import Errors: Constrained Decoding for Guaranteed Valid Imports in LLM-Generated Tests (academic, Konstantinos Kitsios, Matteo Biagiola, Guido Salvaneschi, Alberto Bacchelli, 2026-10-30)

**Why it matters:** A named, implemented deterministic technique
(Constrained Beam Decoding with Lookup) that guarantees 100% valid
imports in LLM-generated tests while avoiding the token-budget waste
plain grammar-constrained decoding causes, and quantified gains of 1-7
percentage points in overall test correctness over static and
agentic-repair baselines across Python and Java — a transferable pattern
for any LLM code-generation pipeline with an analogous
grammar-checkable failure class.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41878 · **Library entry:** [2026-kitsios-cbdl-constrained-decoding-valid-imports](../../../library/entries/2026-kitsios-cbdl-constrained-decoding-valid-imports.md)
**Selected because:** High on evaluation, validation and deterministic guardrails (lens 4) — removes an output-error class by construction, with quantified before/after numbers against both static and agentic baselines.

### 2. Language Models Show Conformity Bias in RAG: When the Majority Endorses a Poisoned Claim (academic, Mehrdad Ghassabi, Audrina Ebrahimi, Zahra Zojaji, Amin Milani Fard, 2026-10-29)

**Why it matters:** A controlled, statistically significant demonstration
that a RAG model's misinformation-detection rate drops by more than half
(25%→10%) when poisoned documents form a retrieved majority rather than
a minority — directly actionable for anyone designing retrieval-grounded
research or policy products: multi-document agreement in a retrieval set
is not a safe trust signal, and guardrail design must account for
retrieval-set composition, not just per-document checks.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41012 · **Library entry:** [2026-ghassabi-rag-conformity-bias-poisoned-claims](../../../library/entries/2026-ghassabi-rag-conformity-bias-poisoned-claims.md)
**Selected because:** High on evaluation/guardrails (lens 4) and reliable research products (lens 8) — quantified (McNemar p = 6.04e-7), names a specific adversarial vector most per-document checks would miss.
