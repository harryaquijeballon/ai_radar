### 1. Where Do Multi-Agent Systems Fail? Evidence-Grounded Diagnosis of Collective Mechanisms (academic, Zhengye Han, 2026-09-30)

**Why it matters:** A multi-agent system can break one of its collective mechanisms and still return correct answers, so output checks alone miss failures. The paper proposes diagnostic contracts that tie each violation to the execution evidence needed to establish it. LLM diagnosers found far more violations from internal records than from public outputs. Contract-referencing prompts beat generic ones, which often claimed certainty without evidence, and accuracy fell on independently built systems. For agentic policy simulations, log internal interactions and require cited evidence for failure diagnoses. This is a preprint, and only its abstract was read.
