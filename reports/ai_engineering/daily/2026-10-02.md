### 1. Risk-Aware Adaptive Evaluation: Finding High-Impact Failures Under Limited Budgets (academic, Priyanath Maji and Spandan Ghose Chowdhury, 2026-09-30)

**Why it matters:** Agent evals are costly and failures are not equally consequential. The paper allocates a fixed trial budget with a risk-aware contextual Thompson Sampling policy instead of uniform sampling. With 50 trials (6% of the data) it recovered 86% of impact-weighted failures, against 25% for uniform allocation, and cut wasted trials on never-failing scenarios from 34% to 2.8%. The advantage shrinks as the budget approaches the full scenario set. For policy-simulation agents, this is a way to concentrate a limited run budget on the scenarios most likely to produce costly errors. The numbers are the authors' own, from a preprint workshop paper, and only the abstract was read.
