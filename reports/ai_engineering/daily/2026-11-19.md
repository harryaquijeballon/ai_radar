## Today's developments

### 1. Idempotency Is Not Recovery: A Fault-Injection Study of Language-Model Agent Tool Actions (academic, Song Xu — arXiv preprint, 2026-11-17)

**Why it matters:** Gives anyone building an agent harness that calls tools with real-world side effects a direct, quantified answer to "is idempotency enough to make retries safe?" — no: a controlled fault-injection study isolates which runtime components (action ledger, reconciliation, evidence sealing) are load-bearing for duplicate-free, certain recovery, and what each costs in throughput.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23353 · **Library entry:** [2026-xu-idempotency-not-recovery-fault-injection](../../../library/entries/2026-xu-idempotency-not-recovery-fault-injection.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 5/6 (observability/debugging; reproducibility and governance) — a measured ablation, not just an architecture proposal.

### 2. YUL: Bringing Software Supply Chain Security to Coding Agents (academic, Aman Sharma, Frank Reyes, Martin Monperrus — arXiv preprint, 2026-11-13)

**Why it matters:** Gives anyone operating a coding agent in CI a concrete, measured control against a specific supply-chain failure mode — agents declaring stale, training-data-recalled dependency versions — cutting the stale-declaration rate from 29%/39% to a 93%/96% fix rate across two different agents and six ecosystems.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23314 · **Library entry:** [2026-sharma-reyes-monperrus-yul-coding-agent-supply-chain](../../../library/entries/2026-sharma-reyes-monperrus-yul-coding-agent-supply-chain.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 6 (reproducibility, security and governance) — a reusable, cross-agent control with a clean before/after measurement.

### 3. A Capability Framework for Evaluating Agentic AI Platforms in Regulated Enterprises (academic, Abbas Raza Ali, Muhammad Ajmal Siddiqui, Moona Zahid — arXiv preprint, 2026-11-18)

**Why it matters:** Gives anyone running a vendor bakeoff of agentic AI platforms a quantified warning — a real ten-platform enterprise evaluation found near-perfect internal consistency across its own scoring dimensions (Cronbach's alpha 0.98), evidence that demo-based panels reward presentation as much as capability — plus a reusable gate-then-score protocol as a remedy.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24039 · **Library entry:** [2026-ali-siddiqui-zahid-agentic-platform-capability-framework](../../../library/entries/2026-ali-siddiqui-zahid-agentic-platform-capability-framework.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 4 (evaluation and validation) — the independent-measurement counterweight to vendor-demo scoring this profile's policy-simulation interest depends on.
