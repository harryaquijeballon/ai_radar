### 1. Release: datasette-apps 0.2a0 (primary, Simon Willison, 1 Aug 2026)

**Why it matters:** A directly reusable agent-tool-design pattern: pair a discovery tool (`app_list()`) with an invisible, sandboxed self-test tool (`app_debug()` — a hidden iframe executing JS) so an app-editing agent can verify its own generated changes before reporting them done, instead of shipping unverified output. Applicable to any harness where an agent edits UI-producing artifacts and needs a cheap self-check loop.
