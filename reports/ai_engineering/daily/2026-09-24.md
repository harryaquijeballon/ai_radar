### 2. Agent Name Collision Attacks in Multi-Agent Systems (academic, Adithyan Arun Kumar — arXiv preprint, 2026-09-23)

**Why it matters:** A2A hosts that use a remote Agent Card's human-readable name as a local routing identifier are unsafe by default — six of seven tested real-world-style integrations already do this, producing wrong-peer dispatch. Directly actionable for anyone building or auditing multi-agent systems that ingest external Agent Cards: route by origin-bound stable identity, keep names presentational, reject ambiguous aliases.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27624 · **Library entry:** [2026-kumar-agent-name-collision-a2a](../../../library/entries/2026-kumar-agent-name-collision-a2a.md)
