## Today's developments

### 1. What We Learned by Reproducing 2,200 Papers from ICML (primary, Hugging Face, 2026-08-13)

**Why it matters:** The largest quantified field test yet of AI coding agents doing reproduction work against real published research claims at scale (1,221 participants, 2,226 papers, 35,908 claims judged). The headline lesson — human oversight remained essential even with capable agents doing the execution, and independent teams often reached contradictory verdicts on the same claim — is directly usable calibration data for anyone building an AI-assisted research-reproduction or claim-verification pipeline, the standing policy-simulation interest this radar tracks.

**Source:** https://huggingface.co/blog/icml-2026-open-reproductions · **Library entry:** [2026-huggingface-icml-2026-open-reproductions](../../../library/entries/2026-huggingface-icml-2026-open-reproductions.md)
**Selected because:** high relevance (lens 4/8), verified — headline figures independently corroborated against secondary coverage, the only item today clearing the verification bar.
