## Today's developments

### 1. The Tests That Grade AI May Be Getting It Wrong (academic, Sanmi Koyejo & Sang Truong — Stanford HAI, 2026-09-25)

**Why it matters:** Applies psychometric convergent/discriminant validity testing to 56 widely used AI benchmarks and finds many disagree with each other or fail to measure their stated target — the BBQ bias benchmark, for instance, can be passed by recognizing "trick questions" rather than by lacking bias, making it measure reading comprehension instead. A companion cross-lingual safety study (1.9M responses, 61 model configurations, 10 languages) shows safety operates through a shared mechanism across languages and that translation itself can inject construct-irrelevant difficulty into a "safety" score. Two directly reusable audit methods for anyone deciding whether to trust a benchmark score before a deployment or governance decision.
