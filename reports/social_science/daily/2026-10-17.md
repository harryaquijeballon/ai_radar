### 1. The AI Expectations Gap (primary, The Anthropic Institute, 2026-10-16)

**Why it matters:** A nationally representative US survey (Morning Consult, ~11,000 adults) set against Anthropic staff expectations gives economists a quantified sensitivity range for growth-scenario assumptions: the same task-automation-to-GDP model implies 5.4% 2030 GDP growth from public expectations versus 17.1% from staff expectations. ### 2. How Employment Is Changing at Anthropic (primary, The Anthropic Institute, 2026-10-16)

**Why it matters:** Realised (not survey-elicited) firm-level data on how heavy AI-agent adoption is reshaping hiring composition: junior technical hiring fell from 27% to 9% of technical hires, and the technical share of employment fell from 61% to 56% in a year. A concrete empirical input for the junior-labour-market-displacement debate, with the authors' own conclusion that firm-level mitigation cannot substitute for public retraining/income-support policy.
