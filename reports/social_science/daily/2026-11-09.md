## Today's developments

### 1. AI for Scientific Discovery: How Researchers Use Claude (primary, Lukas Althoff, Johannes Hermle, Ben Jones et al. — The Anthropic Institute, 2026-11-05)

**Why it matters:** A usage-data study mapping 207,049 real Claude sessions
to a measured AI-capability score across scientific fields and tasks: usage
tracks capability (top-20%-capability tasks see 2-4x the usage their
researcher-time share predicts) and is currently concentrated away from the
tasks scientists themselves rate most important (ideation, data gathering).
Gives the productivity-paradox debate a concrete, quantified mechanism, with
illustrative acceleration scenarios (1.35x to 16x by field) a reader could
cite directly.

**Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/research/how-researchers-use-claude · **Library entry:** [2026-althoff-ai-scientific-discovery-claude-usage](../../../library/entries/2026-althoff-ai-scientific-discovery-claude-usage.md)
**Selected because:** High on economic growth/productivity (lens 1) — a
quantified, if illustrative, usage-to-capability mapping, not a demo; scored
with the Institute's standing vendor caveat.
