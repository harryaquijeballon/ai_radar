## Today's developments

### 1. Practice Makes Perfect: Tacit Knowledge, Generative AI, and Returns to Schooling and Experience (academic, Eduardo Levy Yeyati, Darío Judzik, Juan Ignacio Barbiero, 2026-10-26)

**Why it matters:** Gives economists a quantified, falsifiable account of
how generative AI is repricing human capital within exposed occupations —
not just whether AI affects wages, but which component (codified
knowledge vs. practice-based experience) is being repriced, and over what
horizon.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36008 · **Library entry:** [2026-levyyeyati-tacit-knowledge-genai-returns-schooling](../../../library/entries/2026-levyyeyati-tacit-knowledge-genai-returns-schooling.md)
**Selected because:** A pre/post-ChatGPT event-study on CPS earnings data with specific, robustness-checked coefficients (schooling return −0.55 log points; experience-return crossing point at 18.1 years) directly usable by researchers studying AI's effect on wage structure (lens 1).
