### 1. How Might Fiscal Policy Respond to the Rise of Artificial Intelligence? (academic — NBER Working Paper 35437, Dynan, Elmendorf & Sheiner, July 2026)

**Why it matters:** Shifts the AI-macro conversation from *how much* AI adds to growth toward *what the state does about it* — building scenarios over productivity, inequality, displacement, and capital's income share, then testing fiscal responses (redistribution, worker support, capital taxation) for debt sustainability. The authors' scenario-robustness stance is a directly reusable way to argue policy design under capability uncertainty. These are scenario analyses and policy arguments, not empirical findings.
