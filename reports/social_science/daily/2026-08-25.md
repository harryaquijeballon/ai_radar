## Today's developments

### 1. The Nvidia-Sized Hole in US GDP Statistics (academic, Isabel Juniewicz, Daniel Carey, Phil Trammell, Anson Ho — Epoch AI, 2026-08-24)

**Why it matters:** A specific, quantified reason to distrust headline US GDP-growth figures as a gauge of AI's macroeconomic contribution right now: because Nvidia designs chips in the US but has them made and sold abroad, its value-add falls into a genuine gap in the national accounts. The authors estimate this currently understates US GDP growth by about 0.3 percentage points, widening to almost 2 points by 2028 if unaddressed — a concrete correction term, not a vague mismeasurement caveat, for anyone modeling or citing AI's growth contribution.

**Source:** https://epoch.ai/publications/the-nvidia-sized-hole-in-us-gdp-statistics · **Library entry:** [2026-epoch-nvidia-gdp-measurement-gap](../../../library/entries/2026-epoch-nvidia-gdp-measurement-gap.md)
