### 1. Beyond Headcount and Human Capital: The Effective Cognitive Population as a Decomposable Capacity Unit for AI-Era Planning (academic, Kwan Soo Shin — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-10)

**Why it matters:** Gives growth/development researchers and planners a decomposable, reproducible capacity metric for AI-era cross-country comparison — combining the World Bank Human Capital Index and IMF AI Preparedness Index across 144 countries, with quantified predictive gains over headcount baselines and large rank shifts a planner could act on.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09642 · **Library entry:** [2026-shin-effective-cognitive-population](../../../library/entries/2026-shin-effective-cognitive-population.md)
**Selected because:** High on lenses 1 (growth/productivity) and 2 (development) — a reusable, quantified planning metric, not just a directional claim.

### 2. Environmental and Economic Implications of Artificial Intelligence Data Centers in the United States (academic, Johanna Bolaños-Zuñiga, Alberto J. Lamadrid — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-10)

**Why it matters:** Treats AI compute infrastructure as an economic input with regional, not just aggregate, costs — a framework for assessing where AI/data-center buildout creates local economic and environmental trade-offs, with a specific policy recommendation to calibrate regulation by location and time.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09882 · **Library entry:** [2026-bolanos-lamadrid-ai-datacenters-environment](../../../library/entries/2026-bolanos-lamadrid-ai-datacenters-environment.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 3 (digital technologies and connectivity) — quantifies location- and time-specific externalities and proposes a usable policy framework.

### 3. Estimated Demand for Mega-Constellation Internet Service (academic, Akhil Rao — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-09)

**Why it matters:** A quantified consumer-demand analysis of satellite mega-constellation internet (Starlink) — finding demand growth concentrated in wealthy countries and slower than orbital capacity growth, revising commercial forecasts for the sector downward. Directly relevant to connectivity market analysis and to development narratives about satellite internet's reach.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08851 · **Library entry:** [2026-rao-mega-constellation-internet-demand](../../../library/entries/2026-rao-mega-constellation-internet-demand.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 3 (economics of connectivity and digital infrastructure) — a direct, quantified read on satellite broadband demand economics.
