## Today's developments

### 1. Fairness Drift: Assessing Algorithmic Fairness in a Moving Market (academic, Meng Liu — arXiv preprint, 2026-11-09)

**Why it matters:** A real-world difference-in-differences study of Cook
County's rollout of a gradient-boosted property-assessment model finds
that the model's measured fairness effect depends on how long it has run
since its last update relative to how far the market has moved — "fairness
thus drifts with the market between updates." Any single-snapshot audit of
an algorithmic-government system (tax assessment, benefits eligibility,
risk scoring) risks attributing to the algorithm a fairness change that is
actually a timing artifact.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11783 · **Library entry:** [2026-liu-fairness-drift-property-assessment](../../../library/entries/2026-liu-fairness-drift-property-assessment.md)
**Selected because:** High on political science (lens 7, algorithmic
government) — a quantified, real-deployment finding with a direct
methodological lesson for evaluating any algorithmic-government system.
