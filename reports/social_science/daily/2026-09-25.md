### 1. Why Does Misinformation Propagate Faster? An Algorithmic Perspective on X (academic, Pan Li & Shuang Gao — arXiv preprint, 2026-09-24)

**Why it matters:** Reimplements X's recommendation algorithm against the USC 2024 election dataset and locates a specific mechanism — "engagement fungibility," where instant reactions are rewarded on the same terms as thoughtful engagement — behind misinformation's speed advantage. ### 2. Artificial Societies Benchmark: A Validation Framework for Synthetic Research (academic, Edoardo Chidichimo, Min Jun Jung, Felix P. S. Wallis & James K. He — arXiv preprint, 2026-09-24)

**Why it matters:** Shows that LLM-simulated survey populations can match a human sample's average response while still misrepresenting its variance, trait correlations, and behavior under changing conditions — the exact failure mode that matters when synthetic subjects are used as a research instrument (economic-agent simulations, synthetic consumer panels). The eleven-test scorecard gives a concrete pre-use validity checklist rather than a single aggregate accuracy number.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30030 · **Library entry:** [2026-chidichimo-artificial-societies-benchmark](../../../library/entries/2026-chidichimo-artificial-societies-benchmark.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 6 (AI applied to social-science research) — a concrete, evaluable validity test for synthetic-subject research, not a demo.
