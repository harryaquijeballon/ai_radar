### 1. AI-based matching improves refugee employment in a double-blind randomized trial (academic, Bansak, Hainmueller, Hangartner et al., 2026-09-28)

**Why it matters:** Randomized, double-blind evidence (about 2,000 cases) on an algorithmic public-service tool: +2.2 percentage points in months employed over three years (about 10% relative), +3.9 for 2022–2023 cohorts, +5.2 points employment rate at 36 months.
