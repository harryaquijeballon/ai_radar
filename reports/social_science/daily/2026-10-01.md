### 1. The Macroeconomic Effect of AI: Sizing the Software Engineering Channel (academic, Blumenfeld, Hazell, Lian and Schaab, NBER WP 35793, 2026-09)

**Why it matters:** The paper turns stock-market reactions to AI news into a real-time estimate of AI's macro effect through software engineering: a permanent 32.6% productivity gain priced in by December 2025, a 3.6% GDP effect (6.5% with R&D spillovers), and more than doubled by mid-2026. These are investor expectations, not measured productivity, so cite them as such. The payroll-exposure design is reusable for other occupations. Only the NBER page summary was read, not the full PDF.
