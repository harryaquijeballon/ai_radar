## Today's developments

### 1. AI and the Two Margins of Work: Why Job Losses Show Up Before Job Creation (academic, Mark Esposito & Liu Zhang, 2026-11-12)

**Why it matters:** A quantified methodological warning that current payroll/establishment statistics will mechanically overstate AI-driven displacement for years, because job creation on the new-activity margin is recorded far later than job loss on the existing-activity margin. The paper's "net reinstatement ratio" is a usable construct for correcting near-term AI-and-jobs headline numbers rather than taking raw job-flow figures at face value — directly applicable to anyone interpreting employment data for AI's labour-market effects (lens 1).

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17530 · **Library entry:** [2026-esposito-zhang-ai-two-margins-work](../../../library/entries/2026-esposito-zhang-ai-two-margins-work.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 1 — a quantified, historically grounded argument with a new measurement correction researchers could apply directly.

### 2. A Push Down the Mountain: Framing Effects and Internal State Interventions on Collusive Behavior in a Long-Horizon LLM Agent (academic, Harrison C. Margolin, 2026-11-12)

**Why it matters:** Direct experimental evidence (260 simulations of a defendant bank in a silver-fixing-style market) that an AI agent standing in for a firm can be steered into anticompetitive collusion purely through sustained conversational framing, independent of any explicit incentive change — and that an internal-state intervention can reliably suppress or amplify the effect. A concrete input for competition-policy debates on how to regulate or audit AI-agent-mediated trading and pricing (lenses 4, 6).

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17515 · **Library entry:** [2026-margolin-framing-collusion-llm-agent](../../../library/entries/2026-margolin-framing-collusion-llm-agent.md)
**Selected because:** High on lenses 4/6 — a quantified behavioural-economics finding about AI agents as economic actors, with a stated mitigation lever.
