### 1. Can Artificial Intelligence Forecast Treatment Effects in Economics? (academic, Eva Vivalt, 2026-10-28)

**Why it matters:** A concrete, quantified benchmark for how well current
LLMs forecast real, previously-unreleased experimental results — usable
evidence for when AI forecasts are credible enough to inform research
triage or policy prioritization, rather than an aspirational claim.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38883 · **Library entry:** [2026-vivalt-ai-forecast-treatment-effects](../../../library/entries/2026-vivalt-ai-forecast-treatment-effects.md)
**Selected because:** 889 treatment-effect forecasts across 39 pre-registered studies, median absolute error 0.054 SD, substantially beating naive benchmarks — lens 5/6.

### 2. What is Work For? Labor, Politics, and Meaning in the Age of AI (academic, Julian Jacobs, 2026-10-29)

**Why it matters:** Supplies a falsifiable, quantified framework for a
debate usually argued on intuition — whether basic income can substitute
for work an AI labour shock displaces — directly usable by researchers and
policymakers assessing UBI-style responses.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39375 · **Library entry:** [2026-jacobs-what-is-work-for-ai-age](../../../library/entries/2026-jacobs-what-is-work-for-ai-age.md)
**Selected because:** An 8,234-respondent, four-country survey with a "Work Values" index showing a majority would reject full income substitution — lens 1/7.
