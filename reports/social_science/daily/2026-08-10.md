## Today's developments

### 1. Tabular Foundation Models and the Unity of Economic Behaviour (academic, Victor H. Aguiar — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-07)

**Why it matters:** Gives economists a tested methodology — not just an assumption — for whether foundation-model representations of choice behavior are economically meaningful and transferable across domains (risk, time, loss, valuation, social preferences), plus a reusable placebo-test template (random reassignment) for distinguishing genuine transfer from overfitting. Directly applicable to anyone auditing a foundation-model-based prediction of economic behavior before relying on it in applied work.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06842 · **Library entry:** [2026-aguiar-tabular-foundation-models-economic-behaviour](../../../library/entries/2026-aguiar-tabular-foundation-models-economic-behaviour.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 6 (AI applied to social-science research) — concrete, evaluable, with a placebo control and a structural decomposition a researcher could reuse.

### 2. Measuring Concept Content in Text from LLM Activations: ESG Evidence from Concept Vectors and Linear Probes (academic, Luc Hazenoot, Zhaochun Ren, Amirhossein Zohrehvand — arXiv preprint, 2026-08-07)

**Why it matters:** A cheaper, faster text-as-data measurement instrument for social scientists: a linear probe on a frozen LLM's activations reaches within 0.6 percentage points of a fine-tuned classifier's accuracy on coding a concept (ESG, or in principle any concept) in text, without the cost of building and labeling a fine-tuning set.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07208 · **Library entry:** [2026-hazenoot-esg-concept-vectors-llm-probes](../../../library/entries/2026-hazenoot-esg-concept-vectors-llm-probes.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 5 (LLMs as research instruments — measurement, text-as-data) — a quantified, directly usable applied method.
