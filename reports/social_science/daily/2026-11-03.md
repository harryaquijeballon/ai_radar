### 1. Facts and Figures 2026: 6.3 Billion People Now Online, Yet Digital Divides Persist (primary, International Telecommunication Union, 2026-11-03)

**Why it matters:** ITU's flagship annual connectivity report, published
today: 6.3 billion people (76%) now online, 2 billion still offline, and a

### 2. Same Text, Different Filename: Are LLM Judges Biased? (academic, Sergey V. Popov, 2026-11-01)

**Why it matters:** A controlled experiment finding that three frontier
LLMs score an identical document differently depending only on its
filename label, with a context-specific (not simply own-group) bias — a
credible warning given the paper's own note that leading economics
journals began using an LLM tool for technical submission quality
assessment in 2026. Sharpens the question of whether research processes
built on model outputs can be trusted.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01829 · **Library entry:** [2026-popov-llm-judges-filename-label-bias](../../../library/entries/2026-popov-llm-judges-filename-label-bias.md)
**Selected because:** High on causal inference and empirical methods (lens 5) — a credible, timely warning about a specific LLM-evaluation method failure mode, not a general restatement of AI-judging concerns.

### 3. How Green Is Your Manifesto? Scoring Environmental Positions in the 2024 EU Elections with an LLM Panel (academic, Franco Corti, Sajid Siraj, 2026-11-01)

**Why it matters:** A concrete, openly released computational
political-science method — a three-LLM panel scoring party manifestos'
environmental positions, validated against the 2024 Chapel Hill Expert
Survey across 41 manifestos and 25 parties — reproducing experts' party
rankings while revealing a named systematic "greener than experts" bias.
Directly usable or extensible by political scientists measuring party
positions on other policy dimensions without commissioning new expert
surveys.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02033 · **Library entry:** [2026-corti-siraj-green-manifesto-llm-panel](../../../library/entries/2026-corti-siraj-green-manifesto-llm-panel.md)
**Selected because:** High on political science (lens 7) — a validated, benchmarked, openly released method, not just an application demo.
