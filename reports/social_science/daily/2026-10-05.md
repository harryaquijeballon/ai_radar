### 1. Right Order, Wrong Scale: Auditing LLM Judges for Occupational AI Measurement (academic, Harry Lyu and Neil Thompson, 2026-10-01)

**Why it matters:** Tests 33 LLM-judge configurations against 45,796 worker ratings (O*NET-BENCH). Judges can order responses acceptably but misstate levels: estimated acceptance rates range from 3.0% to 97.9% against 61.1% for occupation-matched workers, and a TF-IDF baseline nearly matches the strongest judge. Researchers using LLM judges to measure AI's task-level effects should validate against human acceptance rates and the aggregates they will estimate, not ranking agreement. Figures are from the abstract; full text not read.
