### 1. Who Keeps the Gains from Personal AI Assistants? Seller Adaptation and the Unassisted in a Language-Model Market Simulation (academic, Haonan Huang and Joey Xiao, 2026-10-05)

**Why it matters:** An LLM-agent rental-market simulation in which executing AI assistants cut adopters' spending by $13.7 per renter-day with sellers frozen, but seller adaptation recovers about a third of that, leaving $8.7. Predicted harm to unassisted consumers (+3.6) did not appear (+0.4, CI -0.6 to +1.3). The design argues for evaluating assistants at market level, with adaptive counterparties, rather than on user outcomes alone. Results are from a simulated market, so treat them as mechanism hypotheses, not real-market evidence. Figures are from the abstract; full text not read.
