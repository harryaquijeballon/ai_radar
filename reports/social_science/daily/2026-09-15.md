### 1. Near-Daily AI Use Among US Adults Has More Than Doubled in Six Months (primary, Amreeta Das, Caroline Falkman Olsson, Yafah Edelman — Epoch AI, 2026-09-14)

**Why it matters:** This is a rare like-for-like, six-month read on the intensive margin of AI adoption: the share of US adults using AI near-daily (6-7 days/week) rose from 8% in March 2026 to 19% in August 2026, more than doubling, while single-day-per-week use fell from 17% to 10% and overall adoption rose from 50% to 56.4%. For growth and productivity research, near-daily use is a more economically meaningful diffusion metric than headline "ever used" rates, since productivity effects plausibly concentrate among frequent users — but the authors flag a survey-methodology change between waves as a comparability caveat worth carrying alongside the headline number.
