### 1. pAI-Econ-claude: A Gated Human-in-the-Loop Multi-Agent Architecture for AI-Assisted Economic Theory Development (academic — arXiv:2607.21268, submitted 23 Jul 2026)

**Why it matters:** One of the first *evaluable* (not just proposed) architectures for AI-assisted economic theory work: a gated multi-agent system tested against an ungated baseline on five matched tasks, with blinded human evaluators preferring the gated version in four of five and a quantified reliability gain (mean failure severity 1.58→1.16; usefulness 2.60→3.10). Gives researchers a citable reliability delta when arguing for or against agent-assisted theorizing, rather than another architecture proposal without evidence.

### 2. The Human-AI Substitution Principle: When will you be replaced by AI in your organization? (academic — arXiv:2607.20781, submitted 22 Jul 2026)

**Why it matters:** A formal, citable framework — the "Human-AI Substitution Principle" — giving precise conditions for when AI displaces human labor in a hierarchy, based on the asymmetry between how humans acquire skills and how AI capabilities scale. Usable as testable hypotheses against real organizational data: predicted outcomes include abrupt (not gradual) transitions in some conditions, flatter hierarchies, and particular middle-management vulnerability.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20781 · **Library entry:** [2026-banerjee-human-ai-substitution-principle](../../../library/entries/2026-banerjee-human-ai-substitution-principle.md)
