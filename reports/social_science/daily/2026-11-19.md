## Today's developments

### 1. AI, Specialization, and the Organization of Knowledge Work: Evidence from Legal Services (academic, Liu Zhang, Mark Esposito, Isabel Yang — arXiv preprint, 2026-11-17)

**Why it matters:** Gives economists tracking AI-and-labour debates concrete, multi-source evidence that a "cheaper expertise" shock reallocates professional-service work along several margins at once — household self-representation, supplier competition, pricing, hours — rather than producing the simple in-house-shift or price-collapse predictions common in AI-and-automation commentary; a quantified difference-in-differences result (self-representation up 6.6 points) triangulated against court, employment, and firm-revenue data.

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23354 · **Library entry:** [2026-zhang-ai-specialization-legal-services](../../../library/entries/2026-zhang-ai-specialization-legal-services.md)
**Selected because:** High on lens 1 (economic growth and productivity) — a quantified organizational-economics result researchers could build on.
