# AI and the Global Productivity Divide: Fuel for the Fast or a Lift for the Laggards?

## Summary

OECD AI Papers No. 51 (December 2025; developed within the joint OECD–Italy Treasury project supporting the Italian G7 Presidency) asking whether AI's productivity gains will accrue mainly to already-fast frontier economies ("fuel for the fast") or allow lagging economies to catch up ("a lift for the laggards").
