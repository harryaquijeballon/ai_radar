# How Organizations Use AI: Evidence from ChatGPT

## Summary
Using ChatGPT Enterprise account records linked to usage data, worker roles, and financial information through March 2026, the authors analyze over 1,500 organizations and 17 million messages. Four findings: rapid growth in usage driven both by new adopters and by rising intensity among existing ones; adoption concentrated among larger U.S. public companies with higher R&D spending; widespread use across job functions, with notably high engagement from early-career workers; and application to diverse knowledge tasks — writing, technical work, communication, and information synthesis.

## Why it matters
A firm-level, usage-grounded (not self-reported) picture of enterprise AI adoption: who adopts (larger, R&D-intensive public firms), who inside the firm engages most (early-career workers), and what for (a broad task mix, not a narrow use case). Useful as a citable adoption benchmark for researchers modeling AI diffusion, firm heterogeneity in AI uptake, or the labour-market implications of early-career-worker AI engagement.

## Verification notes
Read via the arXiv abstract page (2026-08-13). The organization count, message count, cutoff date, and the four headline findings are quoted/paraphrased directly from the abstract. Author affiliations are not stated on the arXiv page, but the paper uses ChatGPT Enterprise's own account and usage data — a vendor-linked (OpenAI-platform) data source. Per the project's vendor-source convention, this distributional evidence should be read as describing OpenAI's enterprise customer base first, and general enterprise AI adoption by inference. Full paper (methodology, robustness) not read at capture; findings not independently corroborated against a second source.
