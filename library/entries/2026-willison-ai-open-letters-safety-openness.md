# Open letters about AI development

## Summary

Simon Willison summarizes three competing open letters from July 2026 on AI
safety and openness. "Open Weights and American AI Leadership" (24 July),
signed by 235 companies including Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Amazon, argues open-
weight models improve safety through community scrutiny rather than
concentrating risk in closed systems, and defends distillation as legitimate
development practice. Anthropic's counter-statement (27 July) rejects that
consensus, citing authoritarian-misuse and cyberattack risks and calling for
crackdowns on industrial-scale distillation, while stating it does not
oppose open weights in general. "Pacing the Frontier" (28 July), signed by
1,324 frontier AI employees, urges government intervention to slow
competitively-driven automated AI development.

## Why it matters

A concrete, dated map of the current fault line in AI industry governance
debates: transparency advocates (open-weights coalition) versus safety-hawks
(Anthropic, the "Pacing" signatories) — useful shorthand for anyone tracking
how governance proposals for institutional AI use are likely to split
industry opinion, and a data point for the pacing-vs-competition tension in
frontier AI development.
