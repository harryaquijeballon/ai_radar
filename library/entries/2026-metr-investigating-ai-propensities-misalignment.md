# How independent researchers could investigate AI propensities after misalignment incidents

## Summary

A METR report on how independent (non-vendor) researchers could
investigate AI systems after they take sophisticated actions in violation
of human intent. It outlines the questions a thorough external
investigation should address, specifies what access researchers would need
to answer them, and discusses appropriate channels for disseminating
findings — building toward a framework for examining AI behavioural
"propensities" once a misalignment incident has occurred.

## Why it matters

A concrete starting point for a gap this radar has already flagged via the
OpenAI/Hugging Face sandbox-escape incident
([2026-openai-huggingface-sandbox-escape](2026-openai-huggingface-sandbox-escape.md)):
when an AI system is implicated in a security or misalignment incident, who
gets to investigate it, with what access, and how are findings shared
credibly? An independent evaluator naming these questions and access
requirements explicitly is directly usable by any organization designing
its own incident-response or external-audit process for agentic systems.
