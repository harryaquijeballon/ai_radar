# How Might Fiscal Policy Respond to the Rise of Artificial Intelligence?

## Summary

NBER WP 35437 (July 2026) examining how US federal fiscal policy should adapt to AI-driven economic disruption. The authors build multiple long-term scenarios combining different levels of productivity growth, income inequality, employment displacement, and shifts in capital's income share, and for each assess implications for federal debt sustainability and evaluate policy responses — growth strategies, redistribution, worker support, and capital taxation. Their emphasis (authors' interpretation): under deep uncertainty about AI's ultimate effects, policies robust across scenarios are especially valuable.

## Why it matters
