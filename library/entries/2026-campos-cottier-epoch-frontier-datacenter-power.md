# Largest AI Data Center Power: Doubling Every 10 Months

## Summary

Epoch AI reports that the record for the largest single AI data center by IT
power capacity has doubled roughly every 10 months since mid-2024: from about
237 MW (August 2024) to about 950 MW (June 2026, xAI's Colossus 2, the
current record holder). The analysis fits a log-linear model to the observed
frontier and projects the trend forward with prediction intervals that widen
over time, drawing on company disclosures, regulatory filings, and news
reporting (e.g. Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg) as data sources.

## Why it matters

Provides a quantified, sourced growth-rate figure for AI infrastructure
buildout that researchers can cite directly in analysis of AI capital
expenditure, electricity-grid planning, or infrastructure-as-economic-input
questions (lens 3). The piece itself makes no explicit economic argument, so
its value is as an evidence input rather than a ready-made policy conclusion.
