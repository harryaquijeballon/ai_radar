# The Rebundling of Freelance Work in the AI Age

## Summary
Compares the activities people carry out in ChatGPT against the work clients
request on the freelance platform Upwork, and examines how clients bundle
tasks into projects. The authors find activities more common in ChatGPT use
tend to lose marketplace share on Upwork, while less-common activities gain
share — consistent with clients substituting AI for the tasks it already
handles well. Within AI-related freelance work specifically, demand is
becoming more varied, and job postings increasingly combine tasks that were
rarely requested together before generative AI. AI-related postings also
carry substantially larger budgets and more hours of work than non-AI
postings, while hourly rates differ little between the two (exact sample
size, time window, and task-classification method not given in the fetched
abstract — unverified pending full-text read). The authors conclude AI is
changing not just which tasks clients outsource, but how they bundle them
into larger, higher-value projects.

## Why it matters
A rare direct comparison of AI *usage* data (ChatGPT) against a real labour
*marketplace* (Upwork), rather than survey self-report or task-exposure
proxies — gives a researcher studying AI-and-labour a concrete, falsifiable
pattern (task substitution tracking ChatGPT-activity prevalence; project
rebundling into larger, higher-value engagements) to test against other
platforms or national labour data.
