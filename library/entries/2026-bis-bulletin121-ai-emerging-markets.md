# Economic impact of AI in emerging market economies

## Summary

BIS Bulletin No. 121 (17 February 2026). AI's productivity gains differ substantially across countries with sectoral makeup and implementation capacity; **AI preparedness** — digital infrastructure, skills, and institutional capacity — determines who captures the benefits. Advanced economies hold near-term advantages, though variation among emerging market economies themselves is significant; infrastructure and human-capital gaps currently constrain adoption effectiveness in many developing countries. Closing preparedness gaps could let emerging markets narrow growth differentials while managing workforce transitions through targeted upskilling.
