# How Do AI Coding Agents Contribute to Software Development? An Empirical Study of Agentic Pull Requests

## Summary

Uses the AIDev dataset to characterize agentic pull requests (PRs) against
human-generated PRs and how their properties evolve across the software
development lifecycle. The study analyzes how merge-rate differences
between agentic and human PRs vary over time, identifies the development
task types where AI coding agents are predominantly applied and how that
task distribution shifts across development quarters, and compares key
characteristics of agentic versus human PRs with implications for software
quality and their temporal dynamics. The authors frame the contribution as
an empirical, longitudinal perspective on AI coding agents' real-world role
rather than a lab benchmark.

## Why it matters

Landscape evidence for teams deciding how much and where to lean on coding
agents in a real SDLC: the longitudinal framing (how task distribution and
merge-rate gaps shift over time, rather than a single snapshot) is useful
context for judging whether agentic-PR adoption in a given codebase is
still in an early, unstable phase or has settled into a stable pattern.
