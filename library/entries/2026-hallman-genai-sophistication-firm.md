# Sophistication in GenAI Use: Field Evidence from a Large Firm

## Summary
Using proprietary prompt-log data from a large firm, the authors observe
713,564 employee prompts and corresponding LLM responses from nearly 4,000
back-office employees across 15 functional areas over eight months of 2025.
They document three findings: (1) senior employees show more sophisticated
genAI use, consistent with domain expertise complementing genAI capabilities;
(2) sophistication varies considerably by function and is highest in
Strategy, Digital Innovation, and Project Management — groups focused on
firmwide strategic initiatives and organizational change; (3) sophistication
shows neither improvement over time nor a lasting improvement following
formal AI training, suggesting sophisticated use is difficult to change.

## Why it matters
A rare within-firm, prompt-level measure of *how well* employees use genAI,
not just whether they use it — directly usable by researchers studying AI
adoption and productivity (lens 1), and a cautionary, evidence-based data
point for the "just add training" assumption embedded in many AI-adoption
policy discussions: sophistication did not respond to formal training in
this setting.
