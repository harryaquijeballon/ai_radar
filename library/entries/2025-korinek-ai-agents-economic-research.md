# AI Agents for Economic Research

## Summary

Korinek's practical guide (NBER WP 34202, September 2025) to autonomous LLM-based agents for economics: how researchers without advanced programming skills can build agents that conduct literature reviews across many sources, write and debug econometric code, and fetch and analyze economic data, managing multi-step research workflows. Through working code examples and conceptual frameworks, it shows how natural-language ("vibe") coding combined with modern agentic frameworks lets economists assemble research assistants quickly, covering the research lifecycle from initial investigation to final analysis. It extends the author's earlier use-case work by tracking the capability shift from chatbots to reasoning models to autonomous agents.

## Why it matters

*(Radar's assessment.)* For the social-science audience: the successor to the author's 2023 use-case taxonomy, and the current best on-ramp for economists adopting agentic workflows — directly transferable to multidisciplinary research practice. For the AI-engineering audience: a worked example of packaging agent patterns for non-developer domain experts — relevant to anyone building research products whose users are analysts, not engineers.
