# Vibe Coding vs. Agentic Coding: Fundamentals and Practical Implications of Agentic AI

## Summary

Review paper (May 2025) formalizing two AI-assisted development paradigms: vibe coding as intuitive, human-in-the-loop, prompt-based conversational workflow; agentic coding as goal-driven autonomous agents with reduced oversight. Builds a taxonomy spanning conceptual foundations, execution models, feedback loops, safety mechanisms, and debugging strategies, and analyzes 20 use cases — finding vibe approaches stronger for prototyping and education, agentic approaches stronger for enterprise automation and refactoring. Concludes that optimal AI-driven development is hybrid: natural-language interfaces combined with autonomous execution in a unified, human-centered lifecycle.
