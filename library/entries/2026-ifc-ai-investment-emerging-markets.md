# Accelerating Artificial Intelligence Investment in Emerging Markets

## Summary

IFC toolkit (May 2026) for investors, policymakers, and entrepreneurs deploying AI capital in developing economies. Argues adoption is uneven due to infrastructure deficits, limited data access, workforce gaps, and weak institutions, and organizes the response through two complementary frames: an **Ecosystem Lens** (stakeholders and innovations from infrastructure to sector applications) and a **Structural Elements Lens** (prerequisites such as data availability and power supply), drawing on IFC investment experience and World Bank operational knowledge, with attention to where emerging markets can "leapfrog constraints."

## Why it matters

*(Radar's assessment.)* A practitioner-grade complement to the macro papers in the development cluster: where the OECD and BIS pieces estimate and diagnose the divide, this one is a deployment playbook — useful structure for any analysis of AI investment conditions in emerging markets, including connectivity-sector angles.
