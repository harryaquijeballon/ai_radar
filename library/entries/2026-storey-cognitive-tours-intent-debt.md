# Cognitive Tours: Making Understanding a Deliverable in AI-Assisted Development

## Summary

Argues that generative AI and agentic coding speed up development but
create two under-discussed kinds of debt: "cognitive debt," where a team
loses shared understanding of how a system works and can no longer
confidently predict the effects of a change, and "intent debt," where the
goals, constraints, and reasoning behind a system go unrecorded. Presents
Cognitive Tours, a web-based prototype for building and following curated
paths linking user needs, design decisions, code, tests, and open
questions. AI-assisted skills audit existing waypoints and tours for
problems or staleness and suggest new ones, while humans choose what
matters, verify the links, and write the narrative — a division of labor
intended to preserve the sensemaking effort that builds shared
understanding. The paper demonstrates the tool on itself and closes with a
proposed evaluation agenda; it is a shortened ICSE 2027 Demo Track
submission, currently under review.

## Why it matters

Names a concrete vocabulary ("cognitive debt," "intent debt") for a
documentation/understanding problem that AI-assisted coding is making
worse, with a working prototype demonstrating one possible division of
labor between AI audit and human sensemaking — useful framing for anyone
designing harness or process controls around AI-assisted development
(lens 2, lens 7), though not yet empirically validated.
