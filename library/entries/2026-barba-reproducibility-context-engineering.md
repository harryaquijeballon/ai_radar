# Reproducibility in the Age of Agentic AI: Context Engineering at the Timescale of a Codebase

## Summary

A short position note arguing that reproducible-research practices *are*
context engineering for AI coding agents: "agents lower the cost of
maintaining tests, commit histories, repository structure, instructions, and
decision records while making their benefits immediate." The author states
researchers remain responsible for verifying these artifacts and the
scientific judgments they encode.

## Why it matters

The note reframes a familiar list of reproducibility hygiene practices as
the specific artifacts that constitute an agent's long-horizon context —
useful for teams deciding what to invest in maintaining as a codebase's
"memory" for agentic workflows over time (lens 2), though it is an argument
rather than a demonstrated technique or evaluation.
