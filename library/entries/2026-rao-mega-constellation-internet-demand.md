# Estimated Demand for Mega-Constellation Internet Service

## Summary
Using SpaceX Starlink subscription data from December 2021 to November 2023, the paper estimates consumer demand for mega-constellation satellite internet service. It finds that Starlink users are highly concentrated in the wealthiest countries, and that demand growth is slower than orbital capacity is being added and more slowly than demand for non-Starlink internet overall. The author concludes the market for mega-constellation internet is less lucrative than previous forecasts have indicated.

## Why it matters
A direct, quantified read on satellite broadband demand economics — a connectivity submarket tracked as a substitute/complement to terrestrial mobile networks. The finding that demand growth is outpaced by orbital capacity growth, and concentrated in wealthy countries rather than under-served ones, bears on both commercial forecasting and universal-connectivity/development narratives about satellite internet's reach.
