# AI-based matching improves refugee employment in a double-blind randomized trial

## Summary

The study randomly assigned about 2,000 refugee cases (January 2020–June 2023) between algorithmic placement recommendations and the conventional placement method used by Swiss migration authorities; placement officers and refugees were blind to assignment and both arms kept identical quota constraints. Per the arXiv abstract page: employment over three years rose by 2.2 percentage points in months employed (about 10% relative), 3.9 points for 2022–2023 cohorts, and the employment rate at 36 months rose 5.2 points, described as comparable to hundreds of hours of intensive language training. The trial began just before COVID-19 disrupted labour markets, which may limit generalizability.

## Why it matters

Rare randomized evidence on an algorithmic tool in a live public service; a template for evaluating algorithmic policy interventions, with effect sizes researchers can cite.
