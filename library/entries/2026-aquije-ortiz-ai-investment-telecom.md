# Are we seeing another dot-com moment? What the AI investment cycle means for telecom

## Summary

GSMA Intelligence analysis (26 Feb 2026) comparing today's AI-driven market surge with the late-1990s dot-com boom by decomposing equity returns into dividends, valuation expansion, and earnings growth. Findings as presented: unlike the dot-com era, where valuation expansion dominated, the current cycle shows stronger underlying profitability — a more fundamentally grounded rally — but corporate leverage is substantially higher today across the S&P 500 and technology sectors, adding financial risk if earnings decelerate. The AI cycle is infrastructure-intensive (cloud, semiconductors, data centres, connectivity), positioning telecom operators as central beneficiaries, yet higher bond yields raise capital costs versus previous booms, constraining already-leveraged operators. Conclusion: structural network-demand opportunity, to be balanced against balance-sheet discipline.
