# The Macroeconomic Effect of AI: Sizing the Software Engineering Channel

## Summary

The paper estimates AI's economic effect through software-engineering productivity, using financial-market data. It estimates each firm's stock-return sensitivity to an AI stock-market index and how that sensitivity varies with the share of firm payroll in software engineering, yielding a forward-looking measure available in real time. Reported results (per the NBER page): from November 2022 through December 2025, markets priced in a permanent 32.6% productivity increase in software engineering; the baseline GDP effect is 3.6%, rising to 6.5% when R&D spillovers are included; by mid-2026 the productivity and GDP effects had more than doubled relative to end-2025.

## Why it matters

It offers a market-expectations benchmark for AI-and-growth claims that does not rely on lagging productivity statistics. Researchers can cite the numbers as investor beliefs, not realised productivity, and can reuse the payroll-exposure design for other occupations or sectors.
