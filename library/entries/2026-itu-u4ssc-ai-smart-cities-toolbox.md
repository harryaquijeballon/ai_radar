# Toolbox for People-Centred Smart Cities in the AI Era

## Summary

ITU and UN-Habitat, through the United for Smart Sustainable Cities (U4SSC)
initiative (coordinated with UNECE), launched a "Toolbox for People-Centred
Smart Cities in the AI Era" marking U4SSC's tenth anniversary. It sets out
four core rules for city AI governance: start with the urban problem, not
the technology; keep public decisions under meaningful human control; apply
stronger safeguards as potential impacts increase; and recognize that
deciding not to use an AI system can itself be a responsible outcome. It
offers practical life-cycle questions covering who benefits or is harmed, how
an application is monitored, who is accountable, and when to change course
or stop, drawing on smart-city examples from Helsinki, Finland, and
eThekwini, South Africa. U4SSC is also releasing companion publications on
urban AI governance, measuring people-centred digital transformation,
digital-public-infrastructure security, city data platforms, anticipatory
governance, and data-enabled destination management.

## Why it matters

A citable, concrete checklist for anyone analyzing or advising on municipal
or national AI-in-government deployment decisions — the "deciding not to
use AI can be responsible" framing and the life-cycle accountability
questions give policy researchers and city officials a usable starting
structure, even though the toolbox itself is principle-level guidance rather
than a quantified comparative study.
